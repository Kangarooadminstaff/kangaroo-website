FAQ

Straight answers.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Kangaroo Logistics holds US broker authority MC 1207189, SCAC code LKAG, and is registered with the Commission des transports du Québec. We’re insured with CNA, and every carrier in our network must name us as additional insured.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide, with a focus on North America: coast to coast across Canada, plus the US and Mexico. Our own reefer fleet covers Quebec out of Laval, and we arrange ocean and air freight with our partners for the rest of the world.

Do you move fresh and frozen?

Yes, it’s our specialty. We haul from −25 °C to +4 °C with food-grade certified carriers, and the setpoint is confirmed before every pickup.

Is there a minimum for warehousing?

No. There are no pallet minimums. We store dry, fresh and frozen product in over 100,000 sq ft of space.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the quote form or call 450-668-0827. Give us the origin, destination, equipment and date, and our team comes back with a price.

I’m a carrier. How do I start?

Sign up on RMIS with the “Start carrier setup” button. You’ll need active authority (MC/DOT or CTQ) and a certificate of insurance naming Kangaroo Logistics as certificate holder and additional insured.
